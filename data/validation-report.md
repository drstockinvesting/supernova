## What These Checks Mean

**Absence-to-mastery correlation** is the flagship measure. If mastery were generated independently of attendance, gaps would spread evenly across every unit and this number would sit near zero. A clear positive value means gaps actually land in the units a student missed, which is what makes the correlation narratives in the UI design possible.

**Behavioral localization** proves the system isolates correlation rather than assuming a struggling student struggles everywhere. The dip should appear in the affected subject and not across the board.

**Tier fidelity** is measured on completed units only. The unit currently in progress legitimately has thin evidence for every student in February, which would otherwise blur the distinction between tiers.

**Transfer honesty** confirms the generator records missing history as a data quality flag rather than inventing a past to fill the gap.
